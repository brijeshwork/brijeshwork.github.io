About My Engineering Journey

Over the past decade, I have operated as a hands-on full-stack product engineer, technical consultant, and product creator. My core focus is helping startup founders and business leaders turn early product concepts into production-ready software systems without the friction or cost of building large internal engineering departments.

My technical background spans modern backend frameworks (Laravel, Python, Core PHP), enterprise e-commerce platforms (Magento 2, WooCommerce), cloud infrastructure (AWS EC2, Redis, Varnish), and front-end user experience engineering.
