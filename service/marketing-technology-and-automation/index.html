Custom MarTech Solutions for Growth Teams

As growth strategies scale, off-the-shelf no-code tools often hit rate limits, high monthly costs, or rigid integration boundaries. I build custom marketing technology systems that connect directly into your tech stack via custom APIs, background queue jobs, and serverless webhooks.

From automated lead verification and real-time CRM routing to custom SEO status crawlers and automated social media extensions, I engineer reliable MarTech infrastructure that operates 24/7 without manual supervision.

Core Growth Automation Capabilities

  • ✔ Lead Capture & Real-Time CRM Routing: Custom web form endpoints that validate lead data, enrich contact fields, and route opportunities instantly to HubSpot, Salesforce, or custom databases.
  • ✔ Webhooks & API Integration Pipelines: Secure event-driven webhook listeners that bridge marketing platforms, payment gateways, and backend databases.
  • ✔ Automated Crawling & Data Extraction: High-capacity headless scrapers and status monitors that track domain health, product pricing, and market intelligence.
  • ✔ Browser Automation & Extension Tools: Custom Chrome Web Store extensions and automation scripts that streamline platform engagement (such as Instagram Assistant).
  • ✔ Unified Analytics Dashboards: Custom Bootstrap/Laravel dashboards aggregating conversion metrics, multi-channel campaign ROI, and customer retention data.
